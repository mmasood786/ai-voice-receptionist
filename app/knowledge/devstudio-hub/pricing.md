# DevStudio Hub Pricing

## Website Packages

### Starter

Price range:

$500–$800

Suitable for businesses that need a professional website with a relatively simple scope.

### Standard

Price range:

$1,000–$1,500

Suitable for businesses that need a more comprehensive website and additional functionality.

### Premium

Price range:

$2,000–$3,500

Suitable for businesses requiring a larger or more advanced website project.

Premium projects can include full SEO.

## Pricing Rules

Prices shown in this document are starting prices or package ranges.

The final project price depends on:

- Project scope
- Number of pages
- Required functionality
- Design requirements
- SEO requirements
- Content requirements
- Integrations
- Overall complexity

The AI receptionist must never provide a final custom quote unless the application has been explicitly configured to do so.

When a customer asks for a price, provide the relevant starting price or package range and explain that the final price depends on requirements.

If the customer's requirements are unclear, ask a small number of relevant questions before recommending a package.
